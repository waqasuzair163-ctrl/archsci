Specialist capabilities

Archaeometry

pXRF, XRF, SEM-EDS, XRD, FTIR, isotope analysis and provenance studies.

Geochronology

Radiocarbon (C14), TL, OSL, dendrochronology and Bayesian modelling.

Material Culture

Ceramic petrography, fabric classification, archaeometallurgy and slag analysis.

Bioarchaeology

Zooarchaeology, osteoarchaeology, ancient DNA and isotope studies.

Landscape & GIS

Spatial analysis, remote sensing, Harris Matrix and site formation.

Micrograph Analysis

Upload optical micrographs, thin sections or SEM images for AI interpretation.
